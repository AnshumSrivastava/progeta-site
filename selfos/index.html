Your data
stays yours.

SelfOS is local-first. Your data lives on your device. Not on a server. Not in a data centre. You — not Progeta Technologies — own your personal information.

Optional cloud sync exists for people who want multi-device access. It is always opt-in. It can always be turned off. Export everything at any time, in open formats.

This is not a privacy policy. It is a design philosophy.
