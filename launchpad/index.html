What happens when a campus
partners with LaunchPad.

01

Awareness Session

We run a campus-wide session that shows students what they do not know they do not know. Live demonstrations of real attack scenarios. No jargon. No slides filled with statistics. Students leave with a changed understanding of how exposed they already are — and what it means to fix that.

02

Skill Tracks

Six 6-month diploma programmes, each covering a distinct cybersecurity role. Students work in simulated environments, not textbooks. Assessment is performance-based. Every graduate holds a verifiable Progeta Technologies credential — not a participation certificate, a proof of capability.

03

Student Chapter

The most engaged students don't just graduate from LaunchPad — they lead it. Student Chapters give high performers a campus leadership role: running sessions, mentoring juniors, and building the institution's security culture. It is how the programme sustains itself.

04

Verified Outcomes

Every programme graduate's credential is publicly verifiable via progeta.tech/verify. We track where students go after LaunchPad. The programme is judged by outcomes, not attendance.
